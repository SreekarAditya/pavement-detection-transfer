# Clause measures under amendment 3

The frozen inventory and fixtures are used directly. The author-form procedure is superseded before any expected answer was written.

The committed source map defines printed-page ranges for each cited locator. The restricted standard uses its numbered subsection. Running page labels and known date or document headers are removed. Remaining numeric values determine M0. The fixed normalization tables cover number words, signs, months and standard unit names. They perform no physical unit conversion. Page ranges containing several clauses can supply context for multiple values. M3 measures the presence of encoded values and units in that cited text. It does not establish logical equivalence of a complete legal or engineering procedure.

The existing fixture requirement is the M1 encoding. A frozen operator counts as expressible unchanged. An unsupported operator with the proposed extension and a committed executable shape predicate is recorded as requiring a new operator. An unsupported requirement without that predicate is recorded as not representable, with the missing predicate capability named.

The frozen evaluator is used without changing its validation order or adding operators. M2 and M4 preserve its raw action and map its explicit missing-evidence and unit-error actions to the amendment's labels. Unsupported operator outputs remain visible as exceptions. M4 includes every unit-mismatch fixture. M1 to M3 denominators follow M0.

The twenty-three worked-example cases transcribe the printed length and spatial-coincidence flags from FHWA-HIF-18-022 Tables 1 and 2. Blank published flag cells mean no violation. They are compared exactly with the evaluator action in the corresponding fixture. The seven vendor-guideline verdicts are the registered comparison set after the original designated FHWA-B-P development example. All seven are labeled as development cases here.

Boundary fixture outputs and their encodings are written for inspection. They have no accuracy denominator.

The SHACL adapter retains nested evidence and individual case targets. It executes the committed shape for each new fixture and each of the twelve registered transfer cases. Constraint validation is reported beside the record capability. Comparing a supplied lower confidence bound is supported by the separate supplied-bound shape. Interval estimation from raw blocks is an external calculation.

Input seals pin the inventory, fixtures, shapes, normalization, published cases, source passages and frozen evaluator files before the clause step runs. A mismatch stops the run. Detailed M3 failure text can contain restricted source passages and stays in the private returned archive.
