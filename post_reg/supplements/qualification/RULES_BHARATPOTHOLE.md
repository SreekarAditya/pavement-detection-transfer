## CPU eligibility

Pin the full versioned ZIP by byte count and SHA-256 before parsing. Pool the official split folders. Retain the published 640 by 640 stretched geometry and single pothole class mapped to D40. The exported README reports no export augmentation. Parse the recording prefix and numeric frame before the hash suffix. Support numeric dashcam prefixes and the documented-inventory Signal timestamps with optional clip suffix. Distinct numeric prefixes are distinct initial recording groups. Signal names with the same timestamp are grouped across clip suffixes; the suffix remains part of the frame family key. Exclude ambiguous families and report them. Collect siblings under their full recording and frame key. Within each family select the first lexicographic full member path without examining annotations. Collapse exact byte duplicates across families and preserve all their video ties. For exact duplicate families choose the first sorted recording and numeric frame key. Treat a present empty annotation as zero labeled boxes and a missing annotation as unknown. Exclude unknown frames. Clip normalized boxes to the image bounds, discard degenerate boxes under one pixel on either side and collapse exactly duplicated boxes within a frame.

Reuse the original perceptual hash and frozen self-supervised copy detection descriptor checks against the Road Damage Dataset 2022 training, validation and test references, all seven earlier evaluation collections, and the three earlier external release candidates. Apply the frozen duplicate cutoffs and remove linked frames. Preserve the entire video and all links detected by the frozen similarity block rule, including links through a removed frame. A recording filename supplies a grouping key. Exact and near duplicate cross-recording links join groups, but independent physical routes cannot be inferred from names alone. Require at least 20 retained groups, 200 unique positive frames and 150 mapped pothole boxes after overlap removal. Record the counts and the exclusion if a gate fails. Operational failures are pending. No inference proceeds on an excluded release.

For an eligible release, split using the frozen image-balanced fraction and seed, keeping groups intact. Record the split, release and adapter hashes. Isolate the held-out annotations as a private artifact before inference.
